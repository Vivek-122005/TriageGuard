## 4. Amounts

Documents with an amount field: 3430. Median $4,137, 95th percentile $30,402, max $629,735.

332 documents fall in the ambiguous $20,000-$25,000 band. Of these, 129 (38.9%) are high-value because they also show a stay of 5+ days or several procedures; the rest are not. A single amount threshold therefore cannot reproduce the labels, which is the multi-signal design M1 section 4.5 asked for.
