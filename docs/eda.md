## 4. Amounts

Documents with an amount field: 3746. Median $18,333, 95th percentile $60,106, max $146,957.

371 documents fall in the ambiguous $20,000-$25,000 band. Of these, 193 (52.0%) are high-value because they also show a stay of 5+ days or several procedures; the rest are not. A single amount threshold therefore cannot reproduce the labels, which is the multi-signal design M1 section 4.5 asked for.
